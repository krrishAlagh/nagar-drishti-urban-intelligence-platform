🎙️ SPEAKER 1 — The Problem & The Paradigm Shift TIME: 00:00 – 01:30 (90s)
Stage Presence: Step firmly to center stage. Confident, empathetic eye contact with judges. Set an urgent, relatable tone.

"Respected jury members — consider the paradox of modern India. We are sending rovers to the Moon, yet every day millions navigate roads with dangerous 15-centimeter potholes, dark avenues under dangling live wires, flooded corridors from burst water pipes, and overflowing sewer lines.

Why? Because municipal governance today is painfully reactive. Cities rely on citizen grievance apps where complaints take 2 to 3 weeks to be verified, or on sporadic manual surveys that cost crores and are outdated before ink dries.

We asked: What if our cities could inspect themselves automatically every hour, without adding a single extra patrol vehicle?

Meet Nagar Drishti (नगर दृष्टि). We transform existing public transit fleets — buses that already travel every arterial road daily — into an active, intelligent mobile scanner network. With custom Edge AI cameras scanning roads, streetlights, water mains, and sanitation, defects are verified and converted into engineering work orders in seconds."

Handoff Cue: "To explain the deep-tech engineering powering this platform, I hand over to [Speaker 2's Name]."
💻 SPEAKER 2 — Technical Approach & Architecture TIME: 01:30 – 03:00 (90s)
Stage Presence: Step forward briskly. Point toward the architecture slide or laptop screen. Crisp, authoritative engineering voice.

"Thank you, [Speaker 1]. Behind Nagar Drishti is an Edge-First, Three-Tier Architecture:

1. Multi-Modal Edge Perception: Each bus carries an industrial-grade Edge AI processor linked to Front, Rear, and Side cameras. Running optimized, quantized YOLO-v8 models at >30 FPS with sub-25ms inference, we don’t just detect defects — our dual-frame geometry calculates precise 3D dimensions: pothole crater volume in m³, streetlight pole deflection angles, water burst flow rates (~800L/min), and sewer overflow spread radius.

2. Privacy-by-Design & Zero Bandwidth Waste: No continuous video is streamed. Face and license-plate blurring happen on-chip. Only verified 10-second incident packages with GPS telemetry sync to the cloud.

3. Automated Engineering Work Orders: Our cloud core performs spatial deduplication — 5 buses seeing the same pothole generate only 1 ticket. It then synthesizes exact materials bills (bitumen hot-mix, dewatering pumps) and auto-routes tickets to PWD, Electrical, or Water departments under strict SLA timers."

Handoff Cue: "Now, how practical and economically viable is this for Indian municipalities? For that, here is [Speaker 3's Name]."
📊 SPEAKER 3 — Feasibility, Scalability & Viability TIME: 03:00 – 04:30 (90s)
Stage Presence: Pragmatic, commercial-mindset. Use deliberate hand gestures when discussing cost comparisons and percentages.

"Thank you, [Speaker 2]. Whenever AI is proposed to government bodies, the question is: 'Who pays for it, and will it work on the ground?' Nagar Drishti is designed for frugal, high-yield Indian municipal economics:

1. Zero Infrastructure Capex: Static smart-poles cost lakhs each and have 50-meter blind spots. Meanwhile, Delhi alone runs 7,000 transit buses; Bengaluru runs 6,500. By equipping vehicles already burning fuel and covering routes, edge camera retrofits cost under ₹15,000 per bus, delivering 98% arterial road coverage every 24 hours.

2. Seamless Institutional Synergy: Nagar Drishti integrates smoothly with existing command centers (ICCC) and grievance backends (CP-GRAMS) via RESTful APIs. When repairs finish, the very next bus passing that GPS point automatically re-scans the surface to close the SLA loop without human bias.

3. Massive ROI: Catching a 15cm pothole before it destroys the road base, or a leaking pipe before it creates a sinkhole, shifts cities from costly reconstruction to affordable preventative maintenance — saving cities an estimated 30% to 40% in annual maintenance budgets."

Handoff Cue: "To summarize our human impact and long-term vision, I invite [Speaker 4's Name]."
