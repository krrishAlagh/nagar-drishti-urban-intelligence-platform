🎙️ Continuous Teleprompter Text (Single-Take Voice Recording)

Every year, hazardous road defects claim thousands of innocent lives across Indian cities. Traditional municipal inspections rely on slow paper surveys or delayed citizen complaint apps that take weeks to resolve.

Welcome to Nagar Drishti—an autonomous urban intelligence and municipal governance platform. Instead of deploying expensive dedicated survey vehicles, we transform public transit buses that already navigate every city corridor daily into a live, intelligent road scanning network.

Mounted on 48 DTC buses, our custom edge AI vision runs optimized YOLO-v8 models at 30 frames per second with sub-25ms latency. As buses drive, onboard cameras automatically detect severe potholes, broken streetlights, water pipeline ruptures, and open manholes. Telemetry streams in real time over WebSockets to our live GIS radar. Spatial deduplication ensures that five buses encountering the same defect generate only one verified municipal incident.

From detection, Nagar Drishti's auto-routing engine assigns tickets directly to the right department—PWD, Delhi Jal Board, or BSES—under strict SLA countdown timers. Field crews don't receive vague complaints; they get complete engineering dossiers aligned with official Indian Road Congress IRC:82 standards, including required materials and excavation protocols. To eliminate ghost repairs, contractors must upload geotagged Before-and-After photo audits, autonomously re-verified the next time a bus passes that exact location.

Powering municipal decision-making is our Multimodal AI Copilot, backed by Google Gemini. Grounded in Indian civil engineering manuals and emergency protocols, the copilot responds to voice and text queries in both English and Hindi. Municipal commissioners can ask for instant SLA breach analyses, material estimations, or crew deployments—with actionable deep links that execute workflows in one click.

Nagar Drishti empowers citizens as well. Commuters get a public transit portal with live bus ETAs and occupancy tracking. Onboard, passengers have a direct Cabin SOS to report reckless driving, phone use, or women safety hazards with CCTV audit logs. Behind the scenes, our Multi-Agency Coordination Hub brings PWD, Jal Board, Power utilities, and Traffic Police into a single shared operational room, ending departmental finger-pointing for good.

By coupling historical accident blackspots with continuous pavement defect velocity, city authorities shift from reactive patching to proactive, preventative road safety. Fully bilingual, privacy-preserving, and built for zero infrastructure capex—Nagar Drishti transforms everyday public transit into the intelligent heartbeat of a self-healing city. Thank you, and Jai Hind!
